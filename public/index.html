Before you ask

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still unsure? Send your question through the contact form and I’ll respond directly.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. They are starting ranges. Final pricing depends on scope, pages, integrations, content and custom functionality.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can modernise an existing site, improve responsiveness, fix workflows or rebuild it where that makes more sense.

Do I need to pay hosting every month?

Not always. Free tiers can suit small MVPs, while customer-facing services may benefit from paid hosting for reliability.

Can you build an admin dashboard?

Yes. Admin features can include content management, orders, bookings, product management, reporting and more.

Can I request ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Hosting management starts from R250/month and site maintenance from R300/month, depending on the support required.
